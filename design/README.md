# GMACH Dragon 100: design records

Engineering design records for the GMACH Dragon 100 micro jet engine (100 N, 0.25 kg/s, 120 000 rpm, 3.2:1 compressor pressure ratio, centrifugal compressor, annular combustor, single-stage axial turbine).

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`turbine/rotor-axial-reduction-2026-11/`](turbine/rotor-axial-reduction-2026-11/) | Axial turbine rotor redesign in CFturbo: axial length 14.45 → 8.27 mm with the machined stator and 24 blades retained. Final settings, iteration log, throat and loading checks, mass estimate, CFturbo reports, open items (disc, FEA, CFD). |

Each design record has a `README.md` with the summary, final settings, method and open items, plus the source exports (CFturbo reports, screenshots) in subfolders.
